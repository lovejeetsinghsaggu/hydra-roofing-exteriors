ROOF REPLACEMENT

Your roof protects your home from rain, snow, wind, and changing weather. Over time, roofing materials can become damaged or worn out.

Roof replacement involves removing old roofing materials and installing a new roofing system to protect your home.

How Roof Replacement Works

  1. Inspect the roof and assess its condition.
  2. Protect the surrounding property.
  3. Remove old shingles and roofing materials.
  4. Inspect the roof decking and replace damaged areas if needed.
  5. Install ice-and-water protection and roofing underlayment.
  6. Install flashing, starter shingles, and new asphalt shingles.
  7. Complete ridge caps and check roof ventilation.
  8. Inspect the finished roof and clean the property.

SHINGLE INSTALLATION

Asphalt shingles are a popular roofing material because they provide weather protection and come in different colours and styles.

Correct installation is important to help prevent leaks and wind damage.

How Shingles Are Installed

  1. Inspect and prepare the roof surface.
  2. Install required roof-edge protection and underlayment.
  3. Install starter shingles along the roof edges.
  4. Lay shingles in overlapping rows according to manufacturer instructions.
  5. Fasten shingles using the specified nail placement and quantity.
  6. Install and seal flashing around roof penetrations and valleys.
  7. Install ridge caps and complete the finishing details.
  8. Perform a final inspection and cleanup.

ROOF REPAIRS

A damaged roof can lead to water leaks, interior damage, and costly repairs. Not every roofing problem requires a complete roof replacement.

Common roofing repairs include replacing missing shingles, repairing damaged flashing, and addressing roof leaks.

How Roof Repairs Work

  1. Inspect the roof and identify visible damage.
  2. Investigate the possible source of leaks.
  3. Explain the recommended repair options.
  4. Remove damaged roofing materials where necessary.
  5. Replace shingles or repair flashing as appropriate.
  6. Restore the roof's weather protection.
  7. Inspect the repaired area.
  8. Clean the work area.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to common roofing questions.

How do I know if my roof needs replacement?

Signs include missing or curling shingles, repeated leaks, and significant roof wear. A professional inspection can help determine whether repairs or replacement are needed.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Many residential asphalt shingle replacements take approximately 1 to 3 days. The actual timeline depends on roof size, complexity, weather, and any necessary repairs.

How much does a new roof cost?

Pricing depends on roof size, materials, roof slope, accessibility, and existing roof conditions. Contact us for an estimate specific to your property.

Can my roof be repaired instead of replaced?

Yes, some roofing problems can be repaired. Widespread damage or deterioration may require a full replacement. An inspection helps determine the best option.

What happens during roof replacement?

Typically, old materials are removed, the roof decking is inspected, protective underlayment is installed, and new shingles and flashing are fitted. The project ends with inspection and cleanup.
